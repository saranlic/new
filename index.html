QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about insurance and our services.

What is the best age to buy life insurance?

The best time to buy life insurance is as early as possible. Starting young ensures you get lower premiums and better health-based rates. However, it's never too late to start protecting your family's financial future.

How much life insurance coverage do I need?

Coverage depends on your family's expenses, debts, and future needs. A common guideline is 10-12 times your annual income. During our consultation, we'll calculate a personalized recommendation based on your specific situation.

What is the difference between term and endowment insurance?

Term insurance provides pure protection for a fixed period at affordable premiums. Endowment insurance combines protection with savings, providing a sum if you survive the period plus full coverage if you don't. Each serves different financial goals.

Can I change my insurance policy after purchase?

Yes, you can make changes like updating beneficiaries, increasing coverage, or even switching policies within a grace period. I'll help you understand your options and make any modifications you need.

How long does the claim process take?

LIC typically processes claims within 30 days of receiving complete documentation. We guide you through the entire process to ensure smooth, fast settlement of your claim.

Do you offer consultation over phone or WhatsApp?

Absolutely! You can reach me via WhatsApp, phone, or email. I'm available to discuss your insurance needs at a time that's convenient for you, with no rush or pressure.
